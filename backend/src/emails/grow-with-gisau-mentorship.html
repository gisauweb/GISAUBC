Grow with GISAU

Figuring out university, careers, and what comes after graduation can feel overwhelming, but you don’t have to do it alone. Grow with GISAU is a 7 to 8 week programme that matches Indonesian students at UBC with alumni and upper-year mentors based on your career or academic goals. Mentors come from tech, business, consulting, healthcare, food and nutrition, academia, and more! 🤝

What you’ll get

  • 1-on-1 guidance from a mentor
  • Actionable feedback on your resume, plans, or next steps
  • Real connections within the Indonesian community at and beyond UBC
